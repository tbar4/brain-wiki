# Taking Literature Notes

Literature notes is a form of practice that will give us feedback of our understanding of a topic or subject, or lack thereof. It will let us know if we need to go back to the text to refine our understanding or update it all together. These should be rather informal summaries about media you have consumed. Make a quick note in your own words on something in your media that is interesting to you. Worry about processing it fully later. Luhmen could fit entire books onto a single notecard, but you can do a source card per chapter or article. End up creating 3-6 permanent notes per literature card.
