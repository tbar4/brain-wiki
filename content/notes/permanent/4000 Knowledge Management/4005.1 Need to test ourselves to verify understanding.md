# Need to test ourselves to verify understanding

Learning is understanding and the best way to test our understanding is to constantly test ourselves on the knowledge in our slip box. We may find that our understanding has improved or changed, and thus we need to reorganize or update or add items in our slip box to fit our new understanding.
