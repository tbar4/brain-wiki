# Making Projects Manageable

Motivation comes from making consistent progress and getting victories through that progress [[4001 Building Your Second Brain#^7d18a2|(21)]]. A good idea is to break responsibilities into shorter term projects in order to increase turn [[4001 Building Your Second Brain#^1ba83d|(22)]] otherwise if you don't see progress you may start getting demoralized and [[4002.1.1 Procrastination|procrastinating]] [[4001 Building Your Second Brain#^7ea5a4|(23)]].
