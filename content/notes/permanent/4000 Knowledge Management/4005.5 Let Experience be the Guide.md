# Let Experience be the Guide

Experts see recognizable patters in their field and act and are guided by their experience. Beginners tend to focus more on thinking than experts because they haven't yet gained that wisdom of experience. Beginners can accelerate their path to experts by writing and reflecting upon their experiences.
