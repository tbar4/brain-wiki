# Information isn't a luxury, it is a key to survival

Information is the basis for any good decision and is required for survival, otherwise we are just acting upon a guess, but even guesses require prior knowledge to make a good decision. Imagine I come up with a game called "FjordBoard" and tell you to pick a number. Without any informational context, you potentially have the overwhelming odds stacked against you to win.
