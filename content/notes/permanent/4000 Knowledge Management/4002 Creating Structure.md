# Creating Structure

If you create a good structure for your tasks, it is something that you can rely on. It allows you to release the burden of trying to remember everything and instead focus on the important things which is the content, arguments, and ideas. A good structure enables flow, which allows you to forgot about time as the work has become effortless.
