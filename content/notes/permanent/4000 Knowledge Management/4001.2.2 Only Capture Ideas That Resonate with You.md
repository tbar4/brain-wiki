# Only Capture Ideas That Resonate with You


previous note: [[4001.2 The CODE Method]]
related note(s):
- 

It is important to only capture the ideas that resonate with you on an intuitive level. Do not put any analytical thought into the process (i.e. I think I should capture this note because it might be important). It is the notes and thoughts that resonate that will drive your creativity and make your second brain useful.
