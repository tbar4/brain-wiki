# Write Notes in Your Own Words

You are much more likely to remember something that you write down in your own words. It is your version of regarding the information. The writing process improves your thinking[4]. The best way to do this is to use the [[4001.2.1.1 Progressive Summarization Technique|Progressive Summarization Technique]][6]. You need to subscribe to the philosophy that you are a [[4003.8 Be a Note Giver, Not Taker|note-giver not a note-taker]][8].
