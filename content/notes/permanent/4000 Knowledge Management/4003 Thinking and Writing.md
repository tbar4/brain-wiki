# Thinking and Writing

“One cannot think without writing.” – Niklas Luhmann 

Writing is not what follows research, learning or studying, it is the medium of all this work. People struggle with writing because they believe it starts with a blank page. Good writing is based on note taking, all you need to do is transfer your notes and then connect them logically.
