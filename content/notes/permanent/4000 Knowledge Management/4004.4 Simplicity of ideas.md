# Simplicity of ideas

It is the simplicity of ideas that makes them powerful. The phrase big things come in small packages is highly applicable, or the snowball effect. Ideas don't need to be huge, they need to have a focus and understanding at the fundamental level. The more complex and idea, the more difficult it is to rationalize and work on.
