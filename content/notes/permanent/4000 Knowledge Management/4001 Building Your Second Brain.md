# Building Your Second Brain

The 3 stages of personal knowledge management are remembering, connecting ideas, and creating the relationships[1]. There are a couple key ideas for knowledge management for note taking and building your Second Brain. In order to help you organize your second brain there is the [[4001.1 The PARA Method|PARA Method]][2]. Within this organizational system, you will want to take notes, and the best method for taking notes is to utilize [[4001.2 The CODE Method|CODE]][3].

Why do we even need a Second Brain? In today's world we are overwhelmed with information now more than ever, but what do we do with all of this info? While it is true a lot of it is useless, we need a way to properly organize and distill the important stuff. [[4003.7 Information isn't a luxury, it is a key to survival|Information may seem like a luxury, but it is key to survival]][9].
