# Be a Note Giver, Not Taker


previous note: [[4001.2.1 Write Notes in Your Own Words]]
related note(s):
- 

Think of yourself as a "note-giver" and not a "not-taker". Make sure that your notes are easy to understand and how they will be useful for your future self.
