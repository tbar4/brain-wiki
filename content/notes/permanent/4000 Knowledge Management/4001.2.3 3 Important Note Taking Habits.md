# 3 Important Note Taking Habits

In order to take full advantage of your second brain you need to have the following habits[5]:
- **utilize checklists** - this will help ensure you start and finish your projects in a consistent way
- **weekly and monthly reviews** - periodically review your work and life to see if you need to change anything
- **noticing habits** - notice opportunities to edit, highlight, and move notes to make them more discoverable fit your future self
