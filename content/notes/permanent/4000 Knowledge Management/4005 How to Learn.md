# How to Learn

Learning is about building meaningful connections between ideas. Most *learning* that teachers try to do is actually antithetical to actually learning because they prearranging the information into modules/categories/themes taking away the ability of the student to build that understanding themselves. Our greatest learning moments come from spontaneous and simple ideas after building our own networks of knowledge to grasp a subject or topic. We never had the opportunity to translate our understanding into our own words.
