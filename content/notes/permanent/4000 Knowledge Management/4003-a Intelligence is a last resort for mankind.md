# Intelligence is a last resort for mankind
 It is more efficient to unthinkingly fall into routine and use minimal brain power to make decisions, or get trapped in an algorithm of doom scrolling on social media. Not only is it easy for our brains because we don’t have to think, but we also get rewarded with quick hits of dopamine for it.
