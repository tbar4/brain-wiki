# Elites are Cosmopolitan, People are local
Rich can often afford to look at reality from the world perspective as their wealth shields them from a lot of the Harsch world of day-to-day survival. The people are much more concerned what is happening immediately around them as they are the most susceptible to change in disharmony and their communities[^1].
