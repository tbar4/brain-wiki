# 2101.1.a Critical Race Theory
Critical race theory was founded in the universities in the 1970s and the goal was to view everything through the lens of race where white people could always be at the fault. It is not based on data or evidence, but attitudes and lived experience. It has an activist dimension and works to transform society and disregard fax in the process. It determines racism to be prejudice plus power which means only whites can be racist[^1].
