# The Double Speak of CRT
Critical race theory allows people to engage in a sort of double speak from 1984 in which they don't say what they mean, in terms of the definition of the word. For example, when they say justice, they mean, revenge, anti-racism means racism, against white people, and they talked about equality, but it didn't seek equal rights[^1].
