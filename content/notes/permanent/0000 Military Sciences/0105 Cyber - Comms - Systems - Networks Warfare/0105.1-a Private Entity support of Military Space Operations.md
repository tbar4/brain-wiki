# Private Entity support of Military Space Operations

Private sector American companies have contributed to the aid of Ukraine in their fight against the Russian Invasion. Notably, Starlink access was provided to Ukrainian forces that greatly influenced the space operations battleground. Examples included using drones via Starlink to target Russian forces from miles away.
