# DoD has poor technical networks (i.e. data networks)
I'll try and focus on developing faster and faster networks via 4G and 5G. The department of defense stands in a marked contrast. The other networks have to work under conditions and in places other networks do not have to. It makes those obstacles significantly harder to overcome because it's network often built by company who core competencies are creating platforms. As a result, military networks are like the chaotic and outdated mess of old technologies and ideas that inhibit the flow of information. The result is that most platforms and systems in the department of defense do not connect to other platforms and systems, and certainly not easily, quickly, or reliably[^1].
