# Chinese Cyber Attacks on American Military contractors
By 2012, General Keith, Alexander, Director of national security agent and Commander of US cyber command, estimated the United States was losing a quarter of 1 trillion dollars every year to cyber and industrial espionage, much of it by China. He called it "the greatest transfer of wealth in history". Some in Washington joked that all of the multi billion dollar acquisition disasters that plagued the US military were actually part of an ingenious plot to sabotage China when it tried to copy them[^1].
