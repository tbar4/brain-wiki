# US Military's main priority should be more resilient networks
United States military should be focused on building more resilient networks that can function securely, recover quickly, and reconstitute themselves even when under severe attack. Military will compete to build beyond line of site communications that function at greater ranges and software divine communications that can hop across radio frequencies as attacker receipt to shut them down. The new model of military communications will not be built on small members of centralized hubs, but rather will push critical communications functions out to the edge of the vast networks that are physically distributed, more secure, less vulnerable, and more resilient. Decentralization of communication networks will accelerate as military become capable of their own version of cutting the cord[^1].
