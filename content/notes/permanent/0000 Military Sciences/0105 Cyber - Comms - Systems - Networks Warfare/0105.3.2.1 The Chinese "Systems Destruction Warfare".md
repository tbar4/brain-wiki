# The Chinese "Systems Destruction Warfare"


previous note: 
related note(s):
- [[2200 China]]
---
The Chinese operational system is made up of five subsystem; 1. information confrontation, and 2. recon intelligence (scouting) systems; 3. command, and 4. integrated support (battle network) systems; and 5. fire power strike systems. The Chinese PLA sees military competition, centering on deconstructing, enemy, reconnaissance, and strike complexes and assets and deem the style warfare as "systems destruction".
