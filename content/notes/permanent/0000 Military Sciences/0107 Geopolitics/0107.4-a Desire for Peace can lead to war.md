# 0107.4-a Desire for Peace can lead to war
Post World War I the Allied powers were just as focused on constraining their own power as they were with their enemies. This lead to the winners effectively not pressing advantage in the revival of Prussian martialism within Germany. The allies felt if they were to take any action, it would lead to another war, but it was precisely this inaction that led to it.
