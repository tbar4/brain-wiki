# 0107.1-b America can't afford to be isolationist
A world without America is an Asia dominated by China, a middle east that aims to finish off the jews and then descend into war with one another, a Europe not mediated by alliance through America. We would see a second nuclear arms race as countries scramble to protect themselves from what use to be provided by the US.
