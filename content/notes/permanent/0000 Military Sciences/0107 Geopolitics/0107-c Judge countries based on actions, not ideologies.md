# 0107-c Judge countries based on actions, not ideologies
Countries ideologies a lot of times don't align with their actions. For example, the USSR was built on equality and workers rights, an ideology that would be shared worldwide by many, but their actions were that of a totalitarian regime worse than any monarchy that cost 60 million lives and had workers living in fear.
