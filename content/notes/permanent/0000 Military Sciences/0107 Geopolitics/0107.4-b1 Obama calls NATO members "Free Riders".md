# 0107.4-b1 Obama calls NATO members "Free Riders"
In April 2016 in an interview with the Atlantic president Obama called the UK and EU "free riders" in regards to them spending less than 2% of their GDP on defence and relying on America to foot the bill for NATO. Obama threatened UK Prime Minister, David Cameron and told him to contribute more if they wanted to maintain their special relationship.
