# 0107.1-a Historical & Neo-Isolationist America leads to chaos
Critics of foreign interventionism during WW2 tried to convince public intervention in Europe's forever war was Jewish trickery & British scheming. Leading intellectuals felt Nazi Germany wasn't a threat and that if it came down to it, the U.S. could defend the Western Hemisphere. In the way no man is an island, neither is a country in a globalized world, America was attacked regardless and ended up fighting a two front war. Modern day Trumpian neo-isolationism is treading the same path.
