# America needs to develop new strategies for international aid and move away from the USAID model

America needs to move away from the handout oriented and partisan politic pushing strategy of the USAID org and it is a good thing it was shut down. Instead, America should focus on providing valuable tech to countries that will help with improving their quality of life. An example could be AI models that help diagnose disease, or prevent the outbreak of famine, or help to plan communal and political strategies that work in liberal democracy's favor.
