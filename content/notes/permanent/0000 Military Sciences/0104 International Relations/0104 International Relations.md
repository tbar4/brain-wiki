# Outlooks of International Relations

There are three broad outlooks that are used to classify the fields of thought in international relations: 
- **Wilsonian Idealism/Liberalism** - the foundation for this field of thought is the democratic and peaceful relations as the world order. Institutions are the leaders in peace and weapons *cause* war and are not just simply a *tool of* war. The idea is that treaties and international organizations will prevent wars.
- **Marxist Collectivism/Socialism** - These ideals are based on the fact that private individuals/entities that engage in enterprise should be obligate in wealth sharing, ideally by states developing a system for global wealth sharing
- **Hobbesian Realists** - generally assume the condition of anarchy in the international order, and that states and men survive by wits and strength alone. Might makes right, if not morally, then by fact. States are always at war and if not actively then they are preparing for the next war. The most extreme form of this ideology is called "Realpolitik".
