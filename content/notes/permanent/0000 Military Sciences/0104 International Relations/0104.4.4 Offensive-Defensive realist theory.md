# Offensive-Defensive realist theory


previous note: [[0104.4 Hegemony]]
related note(s):
- 
---
A form of real theory that determines the likelihood of going to war based on the chances of success. When presenting an offensive strategy that is likely to be more successful than a defensive strategy, states are more likely to go to war. As of right now, it appears that the offensive has an upper hand in space. But it would be ignorant to believe as of this time you could fight a purely space war.
