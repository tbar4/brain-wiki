# major powers, Star Wars on the decline


previous note: [[0104.4.1 What is a hegemony?]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Many major powers start conflicts when they are on the decline. This is an attempt to assert their power and remain relevant on the world stage. This can lead to the peaking power trap which ends up, causing more to ally against the peaking power than for it.
