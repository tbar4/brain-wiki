# Preemptive vs Preventative Strikes


previous note: [[0104 International Relations]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Preemptive strikes are used in scenarios in which a strike is necessary for self-defense, especially against an impending threat, like troops are masking on your border. Preventative strikes are those used to prevent a future threat or potential enemy. For example, an attack on a nuclear facility of your enemy, that could be used to refine nuclear material.
