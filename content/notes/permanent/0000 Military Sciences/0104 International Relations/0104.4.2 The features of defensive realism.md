# The features of defensive realism


previous note: [[0104.4 Hegemony]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Defensive realists are much less likely to embrace the hegemonic theories of offensive realist. Defensive realists believe that states seek out security, which means that they only expand when they have to for security reasons. They will also reduce their power or retract it if they believe that will bring more security. But arms build up for security could still lead to war if it is viewed as aggressive.
