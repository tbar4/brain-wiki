# Technological S-Curves affect IR


previous note: [[0104.2 Technology and International Relations]]
related note(s):
- 
---
A power in an international system that is on the leading edge of an S curve would benefit from employing realist, international relations policies to protect its power from others. When the S curve is flatter, a state should look to cooperate more as an a liberal IR policy, because the tech advantage isn't as great.
