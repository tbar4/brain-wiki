# Realism has several flaws


previous note: [[0104.1 Realism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
What major issue of realism is that it struggles to account for internal politics rather than a response to actions by other states. It's not good at looking at factors like government, economics, ethnic make up, and military. It struggles to explain changes and long-term cooperation other than for balancing power.
