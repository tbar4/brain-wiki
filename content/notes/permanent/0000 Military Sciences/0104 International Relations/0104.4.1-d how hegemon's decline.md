# how hegemon's decline


previous note: [[0104.4.1 What is a hegemony?]]

---
What major issue that can lead to the decline of a hegemon is it if another state creates a new technology that allows them to challenge the hegemon for control of the system. The second reason for decline is imperial over each in which it is too costly to maintain. The first scenario will lead to the actor with the new technology to start a war, but the second scenario will lead to the hegemon to engage in preventative wars.
