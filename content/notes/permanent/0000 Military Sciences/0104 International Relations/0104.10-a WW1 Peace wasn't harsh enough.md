# WW1 Peace wasn't harsh enough


previous note: [[0104.10 Treaties]]
related note(s):
- 
---
While most view the post World War I Versailles Treaty as too harsh on Germany, in reality it wasn't harsh enough. Within a period of 25 years, Germany was able to rebuild and try to take over Europe, again. Germany actually came out in a stronger position as it allowed them to keep their most intelligent officers to focus on new strategies. Compounding this was the break up of Eastern European empires, leaving Germany's neighbors, small and weak.
