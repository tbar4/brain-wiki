There are four branches of feminism:
1. Standpoint feminism explains men and women are different thus spring different qualities to leadership.
2. Liberal feminism, believes women and men are essentially the same, thus there's no difference in leaders based on sex.
3. Marxist feminism believes women will only gain a quality through revolutionary change, they see class and gender divisions.
4. Postmodern feminism, believe that gender roles are a construct in arbitrary and believe power structures put soft and weak traits on women.
