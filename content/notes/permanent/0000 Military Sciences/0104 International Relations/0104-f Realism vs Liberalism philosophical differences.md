# Realism vs Liberalism philosophical differences


previous note: [[0104 International Relations]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Realists adopt a Hobbesian view on the world and believe that the states will only act in their self interest and only restrained by a strong government. Liberals believe that humans are inherently good, and only are corrupted by a loss of freedom, usually because of the two restricted of government.
