# Marx never accounted for Nationalism


previous note: [[0104.6.1 Marxism in IR]]
related note(s):
- 
---
One major failing, and Marxist theory is that the worker had more ties internationally with other workers than they did with the nobles/elites in their own country. Marx underestimated the power of nationalism, and it was proven powerful by the outbreak of World War I.
