# Realism is the dominant worldview post WW2


previous note: [[0104.1 Realism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Since the end of World War II, realism has dominated the international relations world of thinking. It is currently on the decline, but every other theory has to weigh itself against realism, since it is the standard. It has helped us to understand conflict against and between states, and creation of alliances.
