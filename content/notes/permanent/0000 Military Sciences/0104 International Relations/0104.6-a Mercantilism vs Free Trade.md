# Mercantilism vs Free Trade


previous note: [[0104.6 Marxism & Capitalism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Mercantilism is a system in which a country protects its economical interest through the trade barriers and reducing international competition to the benefit of the nation. Free trade focuses on absolute gains in that any benefit is good idea even if someone else benefits more.
