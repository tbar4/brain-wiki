And Greg miller's book sun in space he defines four years in which we should try to judge the benefit of an IR system. Those include 
1. reducing the likelihood of major war 
2. promoting human space exploration 
3. advancing science and technology 
4. improving standards of living  

These areas, have their base in the US national security, strategy, security, prosperity, values, and international order.
