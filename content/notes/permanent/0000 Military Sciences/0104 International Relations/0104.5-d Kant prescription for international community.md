# Kant prescription for international community


previous note: [[0104.5 Liberalism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The Kantian triad is three sets of rules for international community to avoid war:
1. Embrace republicanism give power to the people, and people are generally less likely to go to war.
2. Federation of states these members should renounce war.
3. Individual should be free to travel and do business anywhere.

Kant said all three are necessary
