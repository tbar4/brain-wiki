# class divisions are inevitable according to Marx


previous note: [[0104.6.1 Marxism in IR]]
related note(s):

---
Karl Marx in his classic "Das Kapital" argues that in a capitalist system that class divisions will be inevitable, or anywhere where achievement is measured in power and wealth. This is most common in capital societies where there are those who exploit workers to create wealth. Marks use socialism and communism interchangeably. Socialism, though, tends to be viewed as the economic system in communism is the political system.
