# what is hegemonic stability theory?


previous note: [[0104.4 Hegemony]]
related note(s):

---
Hegemonic ability theory is when certain public goods would benefit large groups that are so expensive. No one is willing to pay for them. Hegemonic stability theory is theorizes that it might actually have a single actor that is willing to put that bill because it brings stability to the system. Most staple when it has a clear dominant power in an obvious hierarchy. It cannot let rising power challenge the status quo.
