# the efficiency of corporations will play a factor in space


previous note: [[0104.6.2 Capitalism]]
related note(s):
- [[1002 Space Development Theory]]
---
The speed and efficiency at which corporations operate will play a huge factor in the future of space. They will be able to not only react to change faster, but also drive that change. They will be the first to research new technology and may act as a state in future space.
