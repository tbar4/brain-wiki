# The Economics of Marxism


previous note: [[0104.6.1 Marxism in IR]]
related note(s):
- 
---
There is yet to be an example where a state proves better often the long run after implementing a Marxist system. The argument is laborers are paid less than the value of the products they produce, which is unfair. Their system has more in common with mercantilism than liberalism as it aims to protect the worker with quotas and tariffs. They believe power and wealth will always lead to exploitation.
