# realism allows focusing on 1 goal - self interest


previous note: [[0104.1 Realism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Liberalism aims to please as many nations as possible with the idea that appeasement and law will quell anarchy. The problem is that everyone has to agree to a system that puts the ever shifting and conflicting needs of dozens of nations over their own self interest. Even if a country wants to engage in liberal policies these can be viewed as antagonistic and create friction between you and others without any benefit. Acting in self interest is also easier for developing treaties and agreements as everyone understands each others goals. Chamberlain believed he was operating in a liberal order and then Hitler engaged Poland out of self interest.
