# what is critical theory?


previous note: [[0104.6.1 Marxism in IR]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Critical theory can mean a various number of things ranging from challenges to the status quo, to critiques that provide no solutions or believe that no solution is possible. These are sometimes summed up as reflective approaches, which oppose pure, rational choice decision making, and believes in starting from individual interpretations, ideas, and discourse that are neither universal nor a path to knowable truth.
