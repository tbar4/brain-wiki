# 3 types of Realist alliances


previous note: [[0104.4.1 What is a hegemony?]]
related note(s):
- 
---
There are three types of alliances that are prevalent and real theory: 
1. Tethering alliance the attempt to reduce animosity between two adversaries. 
2. Binding alliance, smaller states aligning to have power over bigger states. 
3. Blocking alliance used to prevent two states from allying with each other.
