# All IR systems have anarchy as a foundation


previous note:  [[0104.3 Anarchy]]
related note(s):
- 
---
All international systems have to face the reality that they are fundamentally anarchic. We can pretend like there is an international order, but as soon as national state interests are threatened the system will devolve into an anarchy. Everyone is just operating behind the façade of order until we revert to our natural state of being.
