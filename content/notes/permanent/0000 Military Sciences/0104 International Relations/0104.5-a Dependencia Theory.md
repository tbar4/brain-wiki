# Dependencia Theory


previous note: [[0104.5 Liberalism]]

---
The global North scholars argue that they buy resources from the global south at low prices to produce manufactured goods just to sell back to the south at higher prices, thus exploiting the global south. Many argue there is less investment in the south due to a history of corrupt governments, unstable, economic, system, and internal violence.
