# Technology and International Influence

Technology creates international influence via the cultural and economic infrastructure that it develops. Social media platforms can be cited as an example of this. This also calls upon a form of both soft power via the commercialization of propaganda, and indirect warfare via technological disruption and algorithmic based influence.
