The fall of Empire, gentlemen, is a massive thing, however, and not easily fought. It is dictated by a rising bureaucracy, a receiving initiative, a freezing of cast, a damning of curiosity -- 100 other factors. It has been going on, as I have said, for centuries, and is too majestic and massive of movement to stop.
