# The going concern of international orgs


previous note: [[0104.5 Liberalism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
One argument, liberalist make about the international organizations is they tend to outlast the balance of power that created them, meaning cooperation can exist past status quo changes. Realists use this to argue these organizations just perpetuate status quo.
