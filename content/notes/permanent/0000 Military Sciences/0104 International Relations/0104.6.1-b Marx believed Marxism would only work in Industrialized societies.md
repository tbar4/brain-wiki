# Marx believed Marxism would only work in Industrialized societies


previous note: [[0104.6.1 Marxism in IR]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Marx believed that socialist revolution would/could only happen in an industrialized society where capitalism already existed and was exploiting workers. Russia was able to jump to peasants bringing revolution. China showed a feudal agrarian system, jumping to socialism. Vietnam, Cuba and others didn't go through the capitals phase either.
