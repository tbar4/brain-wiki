# Collective Security vs Collective Defense


previous note: [[0104 International Relations]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Collective defense is a realist viewpoint in which states led to defend one another in the event of an attack on one in order to preserve the status quo. Collective security is a liberal viewpoint in which a broader scope of political threats are addressed and do not always reflect interest of great powers.
