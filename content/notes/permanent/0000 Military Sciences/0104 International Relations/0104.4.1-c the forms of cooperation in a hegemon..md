# the forms of cooperation in a hegemon.


previous note: [[0104.4.1 What is a hegemony?]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The first form of cooperation will be through control and coercion, the Hegemon will compel the other states to cooperate. Second, states may seek out cooperation with the Hegemon to avoid being conquered. The third will be for states to ally against the hegemon to change the status quo.
