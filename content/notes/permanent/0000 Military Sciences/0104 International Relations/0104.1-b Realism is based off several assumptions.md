# Realism is based off several assumptions


previous note: [[0104.1 Realism]]
related note(s):
- 
---
In order for the theory of realism to make sense it maintains several assumptions, it views states as the main actors. The international system exist in the state of anarchy. States are unitary and self interested, thus will act in a way that benefits themselves. States are rational actors in that war is always a possibility so states must always prepare for it. Also, it assumes long-term cooperation between states are unlikely.
