# Hannibal's Indirect Warfare

Hannibal’s campaign over land instead of the sea had more to do allying Gaul against the Romans than Roman Sea Power. This worked and Rome was severely beaten by Hannibal several times, yet Rome still one the War, due to lack of Carthaginian support at home.
