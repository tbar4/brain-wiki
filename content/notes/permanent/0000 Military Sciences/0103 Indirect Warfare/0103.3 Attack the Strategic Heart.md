# Attack the Strategic Heart

When Scipio Africanus couldn’t take Carthage by siege he instead attacked its strategic heart in the center of Africa and forced Hannibal back to Africa. Adolf Hitler made the mistake of trying to strike the morale heart in Russia (Stalingrad) instead of the industrial center in Moscow.
