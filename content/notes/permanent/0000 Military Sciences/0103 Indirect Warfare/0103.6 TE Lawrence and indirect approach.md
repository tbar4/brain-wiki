# TE Lawrence and indirect approach


previous note: [[0103 Indirect Warfare]]
related note(s):
- 
tags: 
---
Tian Lawrence wanted to limit destruction even in small wars in order to prevent prolonged campaigns. He wanted to focus on using advanced technology and focus on the indirect approach rather than massed forces to cause the most devastation to the enemy.
