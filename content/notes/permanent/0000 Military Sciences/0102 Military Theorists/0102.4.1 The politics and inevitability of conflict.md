# The politics and inevitability of conflict

related note(s):

Machiavelli believed that conflict was inevitable and that not being prepared for war was a dangerous mistake. He had a very prescriptive methods for handling politics in a world of conflict. Machiavelli highlights strength in conviction and decisions to ally or be a for, neutrality is a "virtue" that actually is a vice. One has to be both a lion and a fox, the allegorical centaur, half man and half beast
