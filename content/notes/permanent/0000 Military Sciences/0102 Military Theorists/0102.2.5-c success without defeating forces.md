# success without defeating forces


previous note: [[0102.2.5 Defeating the Enemy]]
related note(s):

tags: 
---
The best way to increase the likelihood of success without defeating enemy forces is to engage in operations that have direct political repercussions. These should disrupt or paralyze, gain us new allies, and affect the political scene.
