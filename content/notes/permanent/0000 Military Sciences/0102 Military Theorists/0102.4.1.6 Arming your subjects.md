# Arming your subjects


previous note: [[0102.4.1 The politics and inevitability of conflict]]
related note(s):
- 
---
By arming your subjects you arm yourself. Those who were suspect of you become loyal and the ones who were already loyal become your staunch supporters. But if you try to disarm you show that you don't trust them and this will arouse hatred against you.
