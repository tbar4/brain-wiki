# Creating fear for peace


previous note: [[0102.2.5 Defeating the Enemy]]
related note(s):
- [[0103 Indirect Warfare]]
---
If an enemy has a lightly defended territory, and we are not aiming to destroy the opposing army, and we know that they are not seeking a brutal decision, but they fear it, then we should take that position, if it is enough for the enemy to fear us seeking a brutal decision in this can be a shortcut to peace.
