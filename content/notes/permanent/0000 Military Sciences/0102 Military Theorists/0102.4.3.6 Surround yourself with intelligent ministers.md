# Surround yourself with intelligent ministers


previous note: [[0102.4.3 ruling once power is consolidated]]
related note(s):
- 
---
A ruler will be judged on the company he keeps. Do not surround yourself with flatterers as they are only looking out for themselves. allow ministers to be honest with you only on the topics in which you ask. Too much unsolicited honesty will lead to disrespect. But if he discovers that a minister is withholding the truth, they must be punished.
