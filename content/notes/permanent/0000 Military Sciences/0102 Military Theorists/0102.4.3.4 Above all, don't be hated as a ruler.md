# Above all, don't be hated as a ruler


previous note: [[0102.4.3 ruling once power is consolidated]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The most important quality for a ruler is to not be both hated and despised. A prince always need to be aware of external forces and internal subversion that may work to increase the hatred against him. Princes need to keep their hands clean of the unpopular decisions and delgate those to his ministers. You also need to avoid offending those close to your affairs of state.
