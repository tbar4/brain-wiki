# Why are there so few Theorists worthy of the name? 

The institutions that would generally have the experience to breed military theorists are highly rigid and structured, which are qualities that do not lend itself to thinking abstractly. The institution trains soldiers to follow orders, not ask questions, and asking questions is the basis for good theory. Also, in order for military theory to be tested, war is needed. While less war is good, if there is no war, it is difficult to test theory.
