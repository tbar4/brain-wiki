# "Intelligence" (i.e. info about the enemy) is fickle


previous note: [[0102.2.6 Friction in War]]
related note(s):
- 
---
We should only trust, reliable intelligence, and even then we should always be suspicious. You should use common sense in the laws of probability in decision-making. Accurate recognition is one of the biggest sources of friction and war. This is one of the biggest problems between planning and execution.
