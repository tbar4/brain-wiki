# 3 main objectives in war


previous note: [[0102.2.1 General Theory on War]]
related note(s):
- [[0102.4.2 Pacifying conquered peoples]]
---
The 3 main objectives in war are:

1. Armed Forces - Fighting forces must be destroyed to the point they cannot carry on the fight any longer
2. The Country - Must be occupied or they can raise fresh troops but both don't matter until...
3. The Enemy's Will - Needs to be broken, they need to be asking for peace and population must be willing to submit. 

The sequence of events is not essential
