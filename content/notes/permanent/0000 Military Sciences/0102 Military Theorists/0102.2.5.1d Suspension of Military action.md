# Suspension of Military action


previous note: 
related note(s):
- [[0102.4.1.2 You cannot avoid war]] 
tags: 
---
The idea that both sides suspend war is absurd. Generally, they are waiting for a better time to act. If action would advantage one side, then it is better for the other to try to suspend until it is to their advantage. The second reason for suspension is due to imperfect knowledge of the situation.
