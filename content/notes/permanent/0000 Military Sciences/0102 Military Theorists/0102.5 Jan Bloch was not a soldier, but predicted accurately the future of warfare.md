# Jan Bloch was not a soldier, but predicted accurately the future of warfare
Jan Bloch was not a soldier. He was a banker who was born into a poverty in Warsaw in 1836, but worked his way up to become a wealthy railroad financier in Russian controlled Poland. He was passionate about military issues and for years obsessively studied how the new tech technologies of his era would change warfare. He predicted a far more lethal battlefield than his contemporary could ever imagine. He discovered that the invention of smokeless gunpowder would literally lift the fog of war that hung over past complex and opposing armies would remain dangerously exposed. The only reason he has forgotten is because his major prediction was that large skill war would be so great that no country would find it possible to ever go to war[^1].
