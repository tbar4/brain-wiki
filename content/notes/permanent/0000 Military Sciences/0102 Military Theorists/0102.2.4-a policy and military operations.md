# policy and military operations


previous note: [[0102.2.4 The Politics of War]]
related note(s):
- 
tags: 
---
The political object is the goal, and war is the means of reaching it, and means can never be considered an isolation from their purpose. In this case, policy must permeate all military operations. The fiercer the war, the closer to the political objects and military aims will coincide.
