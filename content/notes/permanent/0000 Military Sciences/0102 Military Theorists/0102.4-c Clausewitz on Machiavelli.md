# Clausewitz on Machiavelli


previous note: [[0102.4 Niccolo Machiavelli]]
related note(s):
- [[0102.2 Clausewitz]]
---
Clausewitz was normally critical of other military strategists and thinkers, but deemed Machiavelli to have very good judgement in military matters. Calusewitz incorporated Machiavelli into his own writing and believed he had the correct concept on the nature of war.
