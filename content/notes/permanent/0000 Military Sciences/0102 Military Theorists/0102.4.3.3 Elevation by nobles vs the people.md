# Elevation by nobles vs the people


previous note: [[0102.4.2.3 Establishing Power]]
related note(s):
- 
---
One who is elevated by nobles into a position of power will find that it is maintained by the people who considered themselves his equal, and he will be unable to control them the way he wants. If he is elevated by the people he will be safe because where the nobles look to oppress; all the people want is to not be oppressed. Also, the people are so numerous you cannot remain safe against them, where nobles are a small group that is easier to eliminate.
