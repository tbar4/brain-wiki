# Combat is the only means in war


previous note: [[0102.2.1 General Theory on War]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Everything in war must derive from combat. Even if you are not directly fighting you are working towards supporting the effectiveness of combat troops. Combat is the only effective force in  war. Being cautious has its place and this doesn't mean charge into combat. We are discussing the means not the end. Meaning combat will need to happen, but when it guarantees the highest success of meeting the ends.
