# You cannot avoid war


previous note: [[0102.4.1 The politics and inevitability of conflict]]
related note(s):
- [[0107 Geopolitics]]
---
The Romans would cut off trouble early because they knew letting uprisings or hostile adversaries to go unchecked would allow them to gain power and experience while Rome did nothing. So the Romans would act swiftly and brutally at the first signs of hostility and descent.

"One does not escape war, they just postpone it to their disadvantage."
