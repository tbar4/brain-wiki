# Being feared vs being loved


previous note: [[0102.4.2 Pacifying conquered peoples]]
related note(s):
- [[0103.3 Attack the Strategic Heart]]
---
It is far better to be feared than loved if you cannot be both. Men sooner forget the deaths of loved ones than the loss of their inheritance. The reason Scipio was mutinied in Spain is because of his leniency, he was loved and not feared.
