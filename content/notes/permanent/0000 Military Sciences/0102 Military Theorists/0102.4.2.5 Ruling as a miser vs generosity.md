# Ruling as a miser vs generosity


previous note: [[0102.4.2 Pacifying conquered peoples]]
related note(s):
- 
---
A prince is better off being known as a miser than someone who is generous. It is okay to give away what belongs to strangers, but giving away your own wealth and you will be in ruin. Generosity will result in being despised and hated as you will get a reputation for rapacity.
