# Occupying Enemy territory


previous note: [[0102.4.2 Pacifying conquered peoples]]
related note(s):
- 
---
In order to occupy enemy territory you need the goodwill of the inhabitants, no matter how powerful your army is (Napoleon in Spain is a good example). You must destroy the old order and then not change the laws or taxes. Don't garrison and instead create settlements by replacing native homes with your own settlers. colonization of enemy territory is a must for occupation.
