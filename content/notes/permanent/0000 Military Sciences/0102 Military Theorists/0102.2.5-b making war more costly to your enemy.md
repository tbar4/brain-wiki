# making war more costly to your enemy


previous note: [[0102.2.5 Defeating the Enemy]]
related note(s):
- 
tags: 
---
The effort expenditure of your enemy consists of the wastage of his forces, our destruction of them, and his loss of territory. There are three additional methods, and those include invasion to cease the territory, not to retain it, but to exact financial contribution or lay waste. Second to prioritize operations that increase suffering. The third is to wear down the enemy.
