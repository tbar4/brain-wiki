# Don't remain neutral


previous note: [[0102.4.1 The politics and inevitability of conflict]]
related note(s):
- [[0107 Geopolitics]]
---
You will get respect when you choose a side without hesitation and this policy is always better than neutrality. If a state is not your friend, they will request that you remain neutral, and if they are your friend they will ask you to be an ally. If you are asked to remain neutral and you do this will be viewed as cowardly. by trying to escape one danger you run head first into another. Be prudent and always choose the lesser of evils.
