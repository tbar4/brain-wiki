# Machiavelli Vice and Virtue


previous note: [[0102.4.3 ruling once power is consolidated]]
related note(s):
- [[5001.1a Christianity is ruining the west]]
---
Machiavelli believed that a prince that really understood how power worked would prefer the "vice" of meanness over the "virtue" of kindness in order to protect the stability of the state. He believed that pursuing purely Christian rule would mean a prince losing his position. Ultimately vices that safeguard are better than virtues that ruin.
