# Mind most likely to display military genius


previous note: [[0102.2.2 Clausewitz Phases of War 1|0102.2.2 Clausewitz Phases of War 1]]
related note(s):
- [[4005 How to Learn]]
- [[4004 Generating Insight]]
tags:

The type of mine that is most likely to display military genius is the inquiring rather than the creative mind, the comprehensive rather than the specialized approach, the calm rather than the excitable head. These qualities are those that should be trusted to command.
