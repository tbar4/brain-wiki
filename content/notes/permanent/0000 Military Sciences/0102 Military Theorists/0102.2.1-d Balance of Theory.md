# Balance of Theory


previous note: [[0102.2.1 General Theory on War]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The 3 aspects of war are:

1. People -  The passions in war must exist in the people
2. Commander - The scope in which the war is carried out and the talent, chance, and courage depends on the commanders
3. Government - The political aims are those of the government alone

Our theory should balance these 3.
