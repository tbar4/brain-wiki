# Definition of War (Object of War)


previous note: [[0102.2.1 General Theory on War]]
related note(s):
- 
---
"War is nothing but a duel on a larger scale". The goal should be to force your opponent into a position in which they are no longer able to resist your force. Thus "war is an act of force to compel our enemy to do our will". So the aim is to render the enemy powerless [1].

(a) To impose our will on the enemy, to do (b) which we use the means of maximum available force (c) the aim of rendering him powerless.
