# Fighting by law and force, lion, fox, and the centaur


previous note: [[0102.4.1 The politics and inevitability of conflict]]
related note(s):
- [[0101.1 Logic of Strategy]]
---
There are two ways to fight, either by law or by force. One needs to be both a lion when needed and a fox when needed. If one is always a lion he gets a reputation as a stupid brute, but if he is always a fox, he gets a reputation as an untrustworthy trickster. This is why greeks used the allegory of the centaur, you need to be half man and half beast, hwich in turn becomes more than the sum of its parts.
