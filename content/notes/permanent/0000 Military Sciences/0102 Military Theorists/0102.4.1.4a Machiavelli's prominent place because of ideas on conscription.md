# Machiavelli's prominent place because of ideas on conscription


previous note: [[0102.4.1 The politics and inevitability of conflict]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Way ahead of his time Machiavelli theorized about conscription to rapidly build a military force to protect the nation. These men were supposed to be from the nation and NOT territorial armies or mercenaries (which Machiavelli despised).
