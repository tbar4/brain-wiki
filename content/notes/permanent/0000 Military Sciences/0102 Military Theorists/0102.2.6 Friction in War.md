# Friction in War


previous note: [[0102.2 Clausewitz]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The friction of war are those elements that cannot always be accurately measured when in the planning phase. Danger is a great element and the friction of war as well as physical effort. Friction is what commanders should be intimately aware of, even though it most often cannot be accurately calculated. Friction is what distinguishes real war from war on paper. It is what makes the apparently easy difficult. It can only be reduced by combat experience.
