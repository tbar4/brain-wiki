# 0102.4a The Themes of Machiavelli in "The Prince"
In *The Prince* the themes of Machiavelli can be broken into 3 major themes:
1) The politics and inevitability of conflict
2) Once conflict has been concluded, how to pacify the conquered
3) How to rule the people once power has been consolidated
