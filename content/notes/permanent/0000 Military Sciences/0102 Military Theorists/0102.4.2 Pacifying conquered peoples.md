# Pacifying conquered peoples


previous note: [[0102.4 Niccolo Machiavelli]]
related note(s):
- [[0104.1 Realism]]
---
Machiavelli is aware of the complex dynamics of different peoples and the political systems in which they are familiar. Pacifying requires a different technique that Machiavelli is prescriptive about (like handling democracies vs oligarchies) but has general principles for all systems (like colonization).
