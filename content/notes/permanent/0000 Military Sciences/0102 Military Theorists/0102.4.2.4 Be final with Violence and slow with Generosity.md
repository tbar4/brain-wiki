# Be final with Violence and slow with Generosity


previous note: [[0102.4.2 Pacifying conquered peoples]]
related note(s):
- [[0104.1 Realism]]
---
When you come into power over a new of foreign state, you need to be quick and final with your injuries and violence. People will soon forget the resentment, but when you slowly and gradually confer benefits and generosity to the people they will maintain a sense of happiness.
