# China investing in Autonomous Weapons technology
In April 2018, a Chinese government expressed support for an international agreement to ban the use of fully autonomous lethal weapon systems. However, the ban did not prevent you from *developing* advanced AI weaponry. In fact, China continues to develop autonomous systems at a rapid pace greater than any other military[^1].
