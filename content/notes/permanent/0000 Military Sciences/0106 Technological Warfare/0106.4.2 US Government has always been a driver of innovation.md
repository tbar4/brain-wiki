# US Government has always been a driver of innovation


previous note: [[0106.4 Innovation in AI & Big Data]]
related note(s):
- 
---
A lot of the innovation that has come in America was first created by government funding via the US military such as jet technology at JPL, Internet at DARPA, stealth tech from the air force, as well as computers, radio, technology, wireless networks, etc., but this has been largely forgotten and more. Focus has been placed on consumer driven tech that doesn't benefit the national interest.
