# Do not base strategy on intentions of the enemy


previous note: [[0106.1.3 Military Strategy of Technology]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The only concern with the present tech abilities of the enemy and not his hopes and dreams. He may waste more time, following him down, dead ends, then developing your own superior strategy of technology. You must be concerned with present and future of the enemy, not an analysis of his intentions.
