# Tech War is an alternative to conventional war


previous note: [[0106.1.1 Dominate Tech, Dominate the era]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Tech war is a substitute to the deploying of forces in a conventional war, or as Clausewitz put it, a way to reduce willpower of the enemy while increasing friction and fog of war for them. It is an alternative to destructive war, not the cause of it.
