previous note: [[0106.2 Logistics & Transport]]

Experts believe that this fourth industrial revolution will be based on digital manufacturing and smart production. One indicator is the slowing shift of manufacturing from advanced to low cost economies, coupled with the rise of using robots and AI for increased deficiency and manufacturing. As well as 3-D printing being able to operate anywhere and cheaply produce custom goods.
