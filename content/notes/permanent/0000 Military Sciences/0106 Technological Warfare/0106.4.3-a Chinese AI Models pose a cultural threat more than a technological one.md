# Chinese AI Models pose a cultural threat more than a technological one
The AI models that China is currently building don't just pose a technological superiority threat to the US, but also a cultural one. This opens up China to influence the world in its favor through soft power. We are seeing broad and rapid adoption in China's DeepSeek R1 model[^1]. Their models also help to push and prevent propaganda about the CCP[^2]'.
