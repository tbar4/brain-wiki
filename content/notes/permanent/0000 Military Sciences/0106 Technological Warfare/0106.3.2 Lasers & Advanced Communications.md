# Lasers & Advanced Communications


previous note: [[0106.3 Signals & Communications]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Lasers can enhance military communication by moving high volumes of information securely, and provide greater security, bandwidth, speed, data, flow, and precision reception than other forms of wireless communication. The data cannot be intercepted, unless the adversary is the path of the laser.
