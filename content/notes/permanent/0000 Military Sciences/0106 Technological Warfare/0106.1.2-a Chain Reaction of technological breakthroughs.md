# Chain Reaction of technological breakthroughs


previous note: [[0106.1.2 Economics of Technological Strategy]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The technological breakthrough of one area can cause technological, breakthroughs, and other areas. For example, materials, physics, and chemical science breakthrough is lead to space travel, which led to satellite system breakthroughs, which led to communications array, breakthroughs, which led to GPS, which led to breakthroughs in how we fight wars.
