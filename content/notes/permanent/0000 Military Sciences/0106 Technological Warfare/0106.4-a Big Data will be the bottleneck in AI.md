# Big Data will be the bottleneck in AI
The decisive factor in AI will be the extent to which big data can flow into the modeling systems to update AI algorithms without the speed and latency of distributed big data getting into AI systems. Adversaries will take advantage. Optimizing every part of the big data supply chain will be critical. From compression algorithms to communications protocols.
