# Technological Warfare Theory


previous note: [[0106 Technological Warfare]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Technological Warfare is the direct and purposeful application of the national technological base and of advantages generated by that base to attain strategical and tactical objectives. It is used with other forms of rational power.
