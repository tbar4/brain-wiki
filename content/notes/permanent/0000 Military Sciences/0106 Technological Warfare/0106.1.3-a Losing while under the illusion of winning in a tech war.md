# Losing while under the illusion of winning in a tech war


previous note: [[0106.1.3 Military Strategy of Technology]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Being dominant in tech war is difficult because the indicators of success are vague and inconclusive because of variability, dynamism, and uncertainty. So, unless the form of warfare is extremely well understood it is possible to be losing while maintaining the illusion of winning.
