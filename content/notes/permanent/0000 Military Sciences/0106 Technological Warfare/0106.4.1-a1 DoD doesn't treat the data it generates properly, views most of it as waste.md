# DoD doesn't treat the data it generates properly, views most of it as waste
Much of the department of defense still treats data like engine exhaust, a byproduct of more important activities, which should regularly discard in large quantities. The bigger problem still is that the pentagon leaders have come to appreciate the importance of data, they have not turned to machine learning to help them make sense of it quickly and at scale, but rather have added more people to try to deal with it manually[^1].
