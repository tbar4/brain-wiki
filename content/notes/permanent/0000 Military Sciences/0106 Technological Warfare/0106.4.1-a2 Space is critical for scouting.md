The speed and range at which weapon systems can be deployed, requires being able to scout and monitor for far larger areas real time, meaning we will need to rely more and more on space systems. Given the criticality of space systems in the battle network, means that emphasis should be placed on neutralizing and denying enemies command of space systems.
