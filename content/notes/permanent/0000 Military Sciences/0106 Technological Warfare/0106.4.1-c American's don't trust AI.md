# Americans don't trust AI

A study done in 2025 showed that the global south trusts AI way more than Americans by almost 30% (~50% compared to ~80%). This does not bode well for the future of American AI if we don't trust it enough to prioritize it, and if we do not dedicate a significant amount of money and research to AI we will fall behind on the global stage in technology and the economy.
