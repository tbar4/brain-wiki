# There are 4 main elements in Military Technological Revolution


previous note: [[0106.5 Military Technological Revolution]]
related note(s):
- 
tags: 
---
Military evolutions research focused on what comes about, and characteristics of military technology revolution, and found four main elements:
1. New or advanced technologies.
2. The application of these technologies to new military systems 
3. Innovative operational concepts.
4. Organizational adaptions.
