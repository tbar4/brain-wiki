# Measure Military production by civilian production


previous note: [[0106.2.1 We are potentially entering the 4th Industrial Revolution]]
related note(s):
- 
---
During World War II, America was easily able to switch from commercial/civilian manufacturing to military manufacturing because the skills and materials highly overlapped. It would be an accurate measure to assess wartime manufacturing based on peacetime ability to manufacture materials.
