# Use small effective forces in warfare


previous note: [[0106.1.3 Military Strategy of Technology]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Instead of spreading highly technical and technologically talented individuals across many obsolete systems, have them focus on small teams that can be dynamic and flexible to keep up with the fast pace of technology. This is similar to the Clausewitzian idea of focusing forces at the critical point.
