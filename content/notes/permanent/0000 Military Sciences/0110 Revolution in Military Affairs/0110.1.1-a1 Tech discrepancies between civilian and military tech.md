# Tech discrepancies between civilian and military tech
The key example in the discrepancies found in a "focus on platforms" without innovation is that the most capable computer on border US military system is the core processor on the F-35 joint strike fighter, which has earned the nickname "the flying super computer". The processor can perform 400 billion operations per second. As a comparison, the Nvidia drive AGX Pegasus can conduct 320 trillion operations per second right on board a commercial car truck. That is 800 times more processing power[^1].
