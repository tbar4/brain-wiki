# Focus on Fundamental Competencies
A few business leaders are able to focus on more than 5 to 6 core competencies, a list of 20 to 30 core competencies means the list was compiled indirectly, yet that is what the Pentagon did in 2001 and office of net assessment narrowed it down to the following five notional candidates[^1]:
1. Air dominance.
2. Under the war.
3. Space.
4. Robotics.
5. Realistic training.
