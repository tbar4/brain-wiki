# American Leaders scared of change
In general, American leaders have made the wrong decisions and have learned the wrong lessons from this experience. They have failed so badly in their previous attempts to change in the wrong ways that they assume that any change is a bad thing thus they try to keep things the way they are. Talks of a revolution in military affairs has been banished transformation has become a dirty word[^1]. This leads to situations where leaders kill innovative technologies in the cradle because they don't understand the applications and are worried to threaten their traditional contractors that they are familiar with[^2].
