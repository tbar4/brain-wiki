# The Most consequential tech is developed commercially
Many of the most consequential technologies are being developed commercially and have no interest in providing them to the US military, either for cultural reasons or for bureaucratic and logistics reasons. It has become extremely difficult to contract and work with the military because of the bureaucracy and red tape[^1].
