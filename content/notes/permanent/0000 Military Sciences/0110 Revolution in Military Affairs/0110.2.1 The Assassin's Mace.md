# The Assassin's Mace
China continues to spend money on traditional military systems, such as ships and tanks, but its priority is to develop what it called "assassin's mace" weapons. The name refers to special weapons that were used in Chinese history to defeat more powerful adversaries. It would be like David and Goliath with the goal of not to beat the giant it came, but turned to unable to fight by confronting its vulnerabilities[^1].
