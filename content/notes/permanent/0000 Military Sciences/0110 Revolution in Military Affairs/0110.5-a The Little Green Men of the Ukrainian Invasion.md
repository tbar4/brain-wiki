# The Little Green Men of the Ukrainian Invasion
Washington was highly surprised with the Russian military invasion of Crimea in 2014. Russia had highly capable weapons, such as electronic warfare systems, communications, jammers, air defenses, and long range, precision, rocket artillery, much of which is better than anything US military had. The little green men were able to use these weapons to devastating effect waging a high speed precision warfare that had long been the purview of the US military alo the little green men could jam Ukrainian drones, causing them to fall out of the sky. They could also jam fuses on Ukrainian warheads, so they never exploded or hit their targets. Minutes after talking on radios, the Ukrainian positions were wiped out. The armored vehicles were identified by unman spotter drones and immediately hit with special munitions that came right down on top of them. They also used fishing skins where they called Ukrainian commanders families, pretending to be the commander, and when the families would call the commander back, they would locate their positions and fire on them. The United States military would not be ready to fight this war[^1].
