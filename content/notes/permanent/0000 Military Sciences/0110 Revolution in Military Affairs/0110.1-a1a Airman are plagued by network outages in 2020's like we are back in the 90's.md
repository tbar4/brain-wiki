# Airman are plagued by network outages in 2020's like we are back in the 90's
When Christian Brose was giving a talk to over 1000 airmen about the revolution and military affairs, one airman stood up and asked how can we revolutionize our military if we can't even get email. He mentioned that the Air Force is constantly plugged by network outages that cut them off from Internet and email regularly. How can we expect to execute missions if we can't even get Internet access reliably[^1].
