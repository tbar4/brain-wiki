# 0108.1-a Military Procurement killing innovation & soldiers
To coordinate movements in desert storm the Air Force needed tens of thousands of Motorola radios that anyone could pick up from Best Buy or Home Depot or a camping store. The requirements put on Motorola before the military could purchase or so stringent that it was cheaper to not sell military radios. Air Force had asked the Japanese military to purchase the radios, then send them to Kuwait. The patchwork used to prevent in the military from over spending was now causing it to overspend on items that could be bought off the shelf at any electronics or outdoor store[^1].
