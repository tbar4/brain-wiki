# 0108.1-b1 DoD needs more of a software focus
Most of the focus in the military today is focused on kinetic weaponry, but with the rise of AI and autonomous systems, there needs to be a refocused towards software as the primary asset for procurement by the military. Without strong software systems, we won't get Intel and our kinetic systems are useless[^1].
