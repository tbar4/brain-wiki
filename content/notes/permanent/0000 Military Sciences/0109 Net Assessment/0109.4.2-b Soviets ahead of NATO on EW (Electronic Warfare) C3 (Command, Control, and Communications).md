# Soviets ahead of NATO on EW (Electronic Warfare) C3 (Command, Control, and Communications)
The Soviets viewed electronics warfare and command control and communications as much more valuable than NATO, and thus were ahead in this regard. American approach to C3 was ad-hoc, fragmented, and tactical, or rather than strategic[^1].
