# The Characteristics of Net Assessment
1. You need to take into account differences in national security organizations, there are different perceptions, similar hardware, different applications.
2. There are bureaucratically, organization, and cultural factors that will result in suboptimal behaviors on all sides.
3. Everyone acts on limited resources and imperfect information.
4. It needs to understand that asymmetry between competitors.
5. Need to think over decades.
6. Multidisciplinary analysis is needed i.e. economic, military, political, etc..
7. That assessment meant to be descriptive rather than prescriptive.
