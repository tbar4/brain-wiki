# Net Assessment is the pinnacle of defense analysis
An assessment utilizes a multidisciplinary approach to capture the dynamics of the national or coalition military strengths and weaknesses for comparison with capabilities of competitors and adversaries [^1] .

DOT defines it as compared to analysis, military, tech, political, economic, and other factors governing the relative military capability of the nation[^2]
