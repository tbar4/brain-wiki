# ONA Responsible for 3 Programs
Officer that assessment was a part of the DOD and was responsible from 1973 for three programs:
1. Net assessment focused on select warfare areas to support leaders of the defense establishment, making decisions for extended competition with the USSR.
2. An effort to correct an underestimation of Soviet defense burden as a part of the GNP.
3. Special tasks like lessons learned after Yom Kippur and Falkland war, prepare projections on future security environments, like space-based missile systems.
