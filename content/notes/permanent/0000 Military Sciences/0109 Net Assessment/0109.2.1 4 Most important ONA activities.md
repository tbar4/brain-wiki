# 4 Most important ONA activities
1. Uncover, promote, and encourage heretical ideas in people.
2. Continue to undertake, long-term analytical campaigns against large difficult problems.
3. Support methodological innovation and experimentation.
4. Focus on actual and identifiable customer, avoid a "whole of government" and "nation must do" assessments.
