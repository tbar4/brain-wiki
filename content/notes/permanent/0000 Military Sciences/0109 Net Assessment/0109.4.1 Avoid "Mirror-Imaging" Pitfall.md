# Avoid "Mirror-Imaging" Pitfall
When assessing the reactions and dynamics of an adversary or competitors, avoid basing the reaction on how you would react, instead of understand the political, economic, cultural, or other reasons that would prevent an enemy from responding and the way you would[^1].
