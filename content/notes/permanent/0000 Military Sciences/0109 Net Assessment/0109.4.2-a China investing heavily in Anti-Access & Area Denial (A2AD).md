# China investing heavily in Anti-Access & Area Denial (A2AD)
China is focusing on developing anti-access area, denial capabilities. The idea is that they would prevent the striking capabilities of forward, deployed, bases, or carrier groups, then deny any reinforcements to the area[^1].  The main goal is to break the American kill chain. The plan is to saturate US bases with more missiles than it can defend against the second phase is long range radar to spot approaching US aircraft. The main goal is to make it harder and cost for the United States to use its most effective weapons and fight the traditional ways in which Washington is growing accustomed[^2].
