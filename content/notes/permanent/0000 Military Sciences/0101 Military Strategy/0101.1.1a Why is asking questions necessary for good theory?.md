# Why is asking questions necessary for good theory?


previous note:: [[0101.1.1 Validating Theory]]
related note(s):
- [[4005 How to Learn]]
- [[4003.5 Becoming more engaged in reading]]
---
Questions will give you direction and purpose. they help to understand the outcomes of a problem. Asking questions is a necessity before you read because it will make you more engaged with the text. Answering the questions will help to solidify our understanding and any gaps in the hypothesis that need to be followed up on. Elaboration is the most successful learning method and elaborating on the answer to your questions will develop understanding. Once we believe have our answers we will need to constantly test ourselves as we may have new answer or context to the question.
