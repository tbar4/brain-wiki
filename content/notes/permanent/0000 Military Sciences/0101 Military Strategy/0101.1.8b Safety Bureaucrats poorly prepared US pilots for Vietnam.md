So many military and civilian bureaucrats were so scared of training accidents that they didn't let their pilots fly in real world scenarios that would have prepared them for Vietnam. This obviously led to high casualty rates that for American pilots compared to the Korean War. Eventually, Top Gun was created for Naval Aviators and numbers jumped significantly in US pilots kill to death ratios.
