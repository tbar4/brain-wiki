# Fall of Empires

All Empires eventually crumble, mainly due to the fact that those stands tend to over-expand and the bureaucracies that run them are unable to adapt to new technologies and in a desperate effort freeze technological development. The United States is currently susceptible to these thoughts. Since we have the greatest military in the world, the general consensus is "why change what ain't broke?" But it is exactly this philosophy of complacency and not being able to see beyond the now that is a trap.
