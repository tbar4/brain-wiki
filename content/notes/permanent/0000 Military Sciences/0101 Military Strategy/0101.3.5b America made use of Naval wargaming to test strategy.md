# America made use of Naval wargaming to test strategy


previous note: [[0101.3.5 American WW2 Strategy]]
related note(s):
- 
---
Naval Theorists were able to derive valuable insights from their wargames and problem plans. It showed before WW2 that they needed to maximize the number of aircraft in their battlefleets. This led to an increase in aircraft carriers and reduces in launch/land cycle times. It also highlighted that it is better to "give" than to "receive" in naval warfare. Locate the enemy before they can locate you.
