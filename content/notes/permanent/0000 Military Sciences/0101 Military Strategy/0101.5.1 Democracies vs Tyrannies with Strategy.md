# Democracies vs Tyrannies with Strategy

Democracies do better with strategy because of the general spread of education and power. Tyrannical regimes may have less barriers and brilliant commanders, but that can only last for so long. Bad strategies in democracies require accountability and are course corrected education is why their strategies tend to be better in the long run.
