# The 6 Major Military Domains


previous note: [[0101.7 Domain Interdependence]]
related note(s):

---
The 6 major military domains are: land, sea, air, space, electromagnetic, and cyber. The latter 4 offer speed of action and extended range. The undersea component of the sea domain offers stealth.
