# Validating Theory

You need to be able to explain your theory and it needs to be able to be backed up by facts and empirical data[1,2]. From this empirical data and past you need to be able to develop concepts, i.e. theory will need to be able to draw from other theories[3]. In order for battlefield (regardless of the environment; land, sea, air, or space) commanders to be successful they will need to draw upon theory to develop their own theories[4]. Clausewitz's approach is one in which he teaches how to think and is descriptive, while Jomini takes the opposite approach and is prescriptive[5]. One to be a good theorist should employ both Clausewitz and Jomini; learn to think and develop new theories as others are disproven and become outdates (Clausewitz), but also learn from those that came before you and also have currently shown success (Jomini).
