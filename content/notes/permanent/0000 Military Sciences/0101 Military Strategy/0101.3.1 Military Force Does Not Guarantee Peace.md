# Military Force Does Not Guarantee Peace

Military victory does no guarantee us peace, history has taught us that. It only means that the military force of the grand strategy is not able to function. An opponent could still rely on economic, diplomatic, informational, or through control of military. Rome after Trasimene and Cannae are examples.
