# Modern War requires a concert of domains

Modern war requires that all domains are able to act in tandem; air, sea, land, subsurface, space, and cyber need to be seamlessly integrated. Modern wars are fought through movement, tech, and logistics. Failing to account for these will allow an enemy to divide and exploit.
