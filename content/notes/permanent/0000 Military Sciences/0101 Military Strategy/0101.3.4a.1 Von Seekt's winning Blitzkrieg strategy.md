# Von Seekt's winning Blitzkrieg strategy


previous note: [[0101.3.4a The Treaty of Versailles left an elite German force]]

Hans von Seekt believed that the bigger the mass the more immobile and harder to coordinate it becomes. It cannot win victories, instead it attempts to crush by sheer weight. He believed future warfare was going to be based on small, highly mobile armies of high quality supported by aircraft [1]. Heinz Guderian then added the element of radios to tanks to better coordinate movements [2].
