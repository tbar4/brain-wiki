# Militaries need a Guiding Vision


previous note: [[0101.1.8 We are in a Military Revolution and US leaders are failing]]
related note(s):
- 
---
In the same way China has its 100 year marathon plan, the US militaries need to have their own guiding vision for the future to work towards to remain focused and strong. It should address two major questions:

1. What are we trying to do?
2. How can we accomplish that far more effectively than we currently are?
