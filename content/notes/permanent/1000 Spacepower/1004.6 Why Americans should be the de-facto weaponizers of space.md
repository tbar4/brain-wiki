# Why Americans should be the de-facto weaponizers of space 

Given America's liberal democratic roots, our people tend to have a natural conscious towards the cautionary route. We have a moral ambiguity about the use of force. We have a natural restraint given our knowledge of our power and fear that we will become our worst selves if we use it too sparingly. While not perfect, America has the potential for working towards perfection. The alternative options for control of space are too dire for us to not take the mantle of responsibility for being the dominant power in space.
