# Encryption as a Single Source of Failure

The Air Force's recent hackathon had a project that showed how easy it is to exploit faulty encryption schemes. Even when it was setup properly it acted as a single point of failure that could easily be exploited. More layers of security are needed than just encryption (Schalk et al., 2022, p. 350).
