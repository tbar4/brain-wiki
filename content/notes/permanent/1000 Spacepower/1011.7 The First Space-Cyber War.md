# The First Space-Cyber War

Due to the reliance on both cyber and space based systems and with the lethality that has come from their usage, the Russo-Ukraine War has been deemed the first "Space-Cyber" war. Warfare's lethality is coming more and more from that ability to gather as much information as possible, disseminate that data as quickly as possible to then use to attack vital points of the enemy force. Warfare is becoming more reliant on information as a weapon.
