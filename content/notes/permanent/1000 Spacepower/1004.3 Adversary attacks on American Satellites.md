# Adversary attacks on American Satellites

China and Russia frequently attack US satellites by Non-Kinetic ASAT means (i.e. Laser ASAT, Jamming ASAT, Cyber Attack ASAT), but are also developing Kinetic ASAT, cyber, Electronic ASAT, Nuclear ASAT, and Orbital ASAT weapons. Even North Korea and Iran have weapons that can attack American satellites. The new entente of axis powers (China, North Korea, Iran, Russia) have been using space based tech to dominate its neighbors and increase its Sphere of Influence.
