# SDT-Expansion

Expansion is when commercial or military assets are moved into an area of space, or a combination of the two. This may be due to key resources or terrain in the area that could help give a state or organization a power advantage. The focus here is securing access through occupation, but not necessarily excluding other states or organizations.
