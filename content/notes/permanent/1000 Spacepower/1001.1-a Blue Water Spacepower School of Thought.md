# Blue Water Spacepower School of Thought


previous note: [[1001.1 "Silent Sea"]]
related note(s):
- 
---
	The Bluewater school of thought in relation to Spacepower emphasizes strategic depth in the view that spacepower should not be terrestrially focused. Instead, it draws on the Naval theory of Blue Water naval dominance and control strategy of the open ocean. It believes that spacepower is best projects beyond the terrestrially domain into space. Brett Ziarnick and Joshua Carlson both advocate for this type of approach when it comes to spacepower.
