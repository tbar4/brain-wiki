# Weaponization of Space

Like every other operating environment space will be weaponized. Since modern nations have become so dependent on space systems for their ground forces, it would be negligent to not have a plan for defending their space based assets. Opposition to the deployment of weapons in space clusters around two broad categories of dissent: that it cannot be done, and that it should not be done. Why is it that weapons in space are so dreadful, but the same weapons on land, on sea, and in the air are perfectly fine?
