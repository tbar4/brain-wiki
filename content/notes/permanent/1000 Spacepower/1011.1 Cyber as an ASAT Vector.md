# Cyber as an ASAT Vector

Being able to disable a satellite by non-kinetic means can pose a significant advantage for countries with limited arsenals, but strong cyber capabilities. The threat is very cost advantageous relative to kinetic or electronic ASAT means and can be done by an individual or group of individuals (Schalk et al., 2022, p. 350).
