# AI in the USSF

Experts agree that AI could be useful for supporting the digital logistics of space systems. It could focus on the weaknesses in anti-jamming by rerouting of satellites to skip a jammed one. It could also be used to forecast usage like network traffic on satellites. More importantly though, we need to make sure that we stay ahead of adversarial AI models that could pose a threat to our space systems.
