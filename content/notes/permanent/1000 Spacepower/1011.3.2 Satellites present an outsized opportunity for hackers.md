# Satellites present an outsized opportunity for hackers
Given the lax nature around satellite cybersecurity, the best way to attack infrastructure in the US commerce system would be to target the systems that entities like banks, commercial enterprises, and payment processors use that rely on satellite infrastructure[^1].
