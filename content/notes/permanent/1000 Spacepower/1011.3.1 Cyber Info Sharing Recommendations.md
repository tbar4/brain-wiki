# Cyber Info Sharing Recommendations

Many researchers agree that in order for the USSF to be the most knowledgeable and cutting edge in the cyber security field, they will need to be less reluctant than other branches to engage in information sharing with the private sector. Protection of modern day military information is parallel to the protection of nuclear weapons.
