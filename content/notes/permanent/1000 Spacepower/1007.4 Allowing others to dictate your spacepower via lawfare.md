# Allowing others to dictate your spacepower via lawfare

States will place limitations on others in order to best benefit themselves when it comes to IR. This is a more Hobbesian view of Politics than a Wilsonian one. OST prevents the weaponization of space, yet, there are only a couple nations that could weaponize space and the antagonistic ones will weaponize it regardless of treaty status. America not weaponizing space is to its own detriment.
