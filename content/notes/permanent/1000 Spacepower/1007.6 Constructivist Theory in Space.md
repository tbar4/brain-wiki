# Constructivist Theory in Space

Constructivist theory states that the world is constantly "under construction" and that all forms of social interaction shift accordingly to what best suits their social arrangements. This is seemingly a "bottoms up" approach to IR in the same way the Zettelkasten is a bottoms up approach to note taking.
