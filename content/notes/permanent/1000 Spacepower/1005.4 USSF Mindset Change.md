# USSF Mindset Change

Currently, the USSF is stuck in a mindset that is built on Industrial Age thinking. In order to stay a leader in space power they need to:
- Develop a new mindset
- Take on more Risk
- Create New Organizations

USSF also needs to not just view itself as a war-fighting domain, but a way to support national power through space commerce protection.
