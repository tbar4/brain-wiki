# John Klein

In Klein's seminal work "Space Warfare", he applies Sir Julian Corbett's "Some Principles of Maritime Strategy" to spacepower, but it doesn't always apply. Klein takes after Julian Corbett in stating that spacepower should be integrated with sea, air, and land forces, but doesn't look at it as a force unto its own.
