# Iridium Satellite Constellation originally shipped with no cybersec
The original creators of the pentagon's iridium satellite communications array believed that the system was so complex that there was no need for any type of cybersecurity on the platform. This was a strong example of the ignorance of cybersecurity in the early days of networked communications[^1].
