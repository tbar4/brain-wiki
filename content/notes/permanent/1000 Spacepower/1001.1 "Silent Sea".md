# "Silent Sea"

Joshua Carlson created the term "Silent Sea" to represent how military theorists should view space. Currently, it is deemed as "the ultimate high ground" but this is a very terrestrial centric view of spacepower leading the a Dolman style "*brown water*" school of thought instead of a more Carlsonian or Ziarnick "*blue-water*" school of thought about space.
