# Chinese Indirect Approach to Warfare - Economic

China tends to engage in [[0103 Indirect Warfare|indirect approach]] to warfare which uses expansion instead of proxy or direct warfare. This is why China is leading the way in economic expansion while the US engages in Military and Scientific means to spacepower. China has and continues to invest in:
- Space Based Solar Power
- AI
- 3D Printing
- Quantum Computing
