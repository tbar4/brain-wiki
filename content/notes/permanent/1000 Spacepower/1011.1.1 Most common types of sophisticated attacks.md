# Most common types of sophisticated attacks

The most common types of sophisticated attacks on satellite systems were;
- DoS (forcing satellites to enter safe mode)
- hardware backdoor (command injections)
- privilege escalation (sending unwanted commands to payload software applications)
- malware
- payload hacking
