# Russian cyberattack on US made ViaSat

An hour before the Russian invasion of Ukraine, the American made ViaSat satellite communication system was hit by a Russian cyberattack. The attack vector was via an unpatched VPN and targeted cells and modems that overlapped Ukraine. This incident was deemed as the most significant cyber attack against a space system in recent history.
