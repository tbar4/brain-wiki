# American Reliance on Space

If American's were to have a major loss to space systems the resulting fallout would be disastrous to both the military and civilians. Without the flow of information that is depended on from space systems, the American economy would collapse and the world's shortly after. The military would be unable to move and have to plan strategies on how to retreat from forward deployments in foreign countries.
