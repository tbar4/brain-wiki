# Astronautics

Astronautics focuses on more of the commercial and [[1008.1 Space Industrial Capacity|industrial applications]] and elements of space. It helps provide a strategic benefit to commercial space power. It covers technology, industry, [[1008.2 Space Personnel|personnel]], commerce, and basing. Both commerce and the military benefit from technology, so astronautics helps build and sustain space power. Astronautics will require large diverse groups of thinkers to collaborate in order to utilize it to its full capacity.
