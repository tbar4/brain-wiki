# Brown Water Spacepower School of Thought


previous note: [[1001.1 "Silent Sea"]]
related note(s):
- 
---
The Brown Water school of thought tends to view spacepower as an aid to terrestrially based land and air power. Everett Dolman is a leader in this school of thought, reasoning that America needs to be very hawkish and use space power to influence earth based power.
