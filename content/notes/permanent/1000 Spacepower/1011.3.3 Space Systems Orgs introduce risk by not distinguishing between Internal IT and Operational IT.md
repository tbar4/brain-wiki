# [[1009 Space Systems|Space Systems]] Orgs introduce risk by not distinguishing between Internal IT and Operational IT
Organizations that create space systems generally don't distinguish between internal IT teams and Operational IT. This means that IT professionals that may have been trained to help troubleshoot network problems on windows are not seen as any different than someone who is trained to understand the vulnerabilities of a space system written in a custom linux distro[^1]. This is exacerbated by the fact the normally cybersecurity is not a line item in mission budgets[^2] Having specialized teams that have better understanding of the mission systems can reduce vulnerabilities through better analysis and protection.[^3]
