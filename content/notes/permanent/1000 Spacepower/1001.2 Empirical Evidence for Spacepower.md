# Empirical Evidence for Spacepower

Mahan had several centuries of empirical evidence to draw upon in order to develop his theory of seapower. Spacepower theorists do not have the same luxury of empirical evidence for spacepower, but do have the strategic theory foundations of the giants before them to start developing the spacepower framework.
