## Where to start

This site launched in one batch, so a "recently added" feed would be fake —
564 pages, one publish date, no real spread yet. That will stop being true
as [[wiki/wiki|the nightly build]] accumulates real history. For now, three
honest entry points:

- **Widest cluster:** [[wiki/evergreen/classical-strategy-and-theorists|Classical Strategy and Theorists]], 151 notes.
- **The argument that started the project:** [[wiki/evergreen/military-technological-revolution|Military-Technological Revolution]] — the two densest concepts in the vault turned out to have no sentence at their own root.
- **What's actually missing:** every hub's `Gaps detected` section reports what the corpus doesn't cover — read one before assuming the corpus is complete.

## About this site

**Split, not summarized.** Every hub is one file, cut by a literal marker.
Above it is argument; below it is a generated index of notes and gaps. A
generator can rewrite the index every night without ever touching the
argument — the split is the entire safety property, not a convention someone
has to remember.

**An archive stamp marks a dormant project, not an invalid claim.** Most of
the 89 engineering principles here came from projects that are no longer
active. The lessons didn't expire when the projects did.

**Absence is content.** A hub that only lists its notes is a search result.
The `Gaps detected` section on every page — an untagged branch, a citation
count of one, a root note with no prose — is what makes it a wiki entry
instead.
